5. What we recommend

Expand installer reach in North AmericaAdd partner installers or mobile install teams for the 171 North American customers beyond 5 km, where only 27% buy.
Lifting them to 51% (the near rate) adds about 41 sales.
Remove the distance costOffer free or flat-fee installation and a remote site assessment for anyone more than 5 km away.
Beyond 5 km: 36% vs 53% within.
Family and multi-car bundleMarket larger backup packages with payment plans to married households over 40 and homes with 2+ vehicles.
These groups buy at 34% to 37%.
Protect the core buyersKeep digital campaigns and referrals aimed at 30-39 year olds, professionals and Pacific customers.
They buy at 54% to 59% and are the cheapest to win.
